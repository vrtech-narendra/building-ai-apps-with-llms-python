# Introduction to LLMs

Welcome to the first lesson of the **Building AI Applications with LLMs Using Python** course.

In this lesson, you'll learn the fundamental concepts of Large Language Models (LLMs) and understand why they are the foundation of modern AI applications.

---

## 📚 Topics Covered

- What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?
- AI Ecosystem
- What is an AI Application?
- Traditional Applications vs AI Applications
- What is a Large Language Model (LLM)?
- Why LLMs are called the "Brain" of AI Applications
- Knowledge Cutoff
- Stateless Nature of LLMs (No Memory)
- Key Takeaways
